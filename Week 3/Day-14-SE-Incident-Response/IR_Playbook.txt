DAY 14 — SOCIAL ENGINEERING INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

1. PREPARATION
• Maintain security-awareness training.
• Define incident-response responsibilities.
• Maintain logging and communication procedures.

2. IDENTIFICATION
• Review phishing alerts.
• Check suspicious login activity.
• Analyze email and security logs.
• Confirm incident type and severity.

3. CONTAINMENT
• Lock affected user account.
• Revoke active sessions.
• Quarantine suspicious email.
• Block suspicious sender domain.
• Preserve relevant logs.

4. ERADICATION
• Remove malicious email or content.
• Scan suspicious attachments in a sandbox.
• Reset affected credentials where required.
• Remove unauthorized changes.

5. RECOVERY
• Restore normal account access after verification.
• Monitor affected accounts.
• Confirm systems are operating normally.

6. LESSONS LEARNED
• Document the incident.
• Identify the root cause.
• Improve security controls.
• Provide additional user awareness training.

DOCUMENTATION
All incident details, actions, timestamps, and findings should be documented for future analysis and reporting.
